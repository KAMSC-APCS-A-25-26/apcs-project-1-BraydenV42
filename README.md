This story is based on a combination of the Sonic Aus Moribund and Epilepsia
The story starts with you in your house. Your friend had recently gone missing and you eventually end up heading to his house to mend your mental health.
After a long and rainy walk you enter his house.
You explore for a bit and end up stumbling across an empty room and a computer with a game on it.
After pressing play you pass out on the floor. Upon waking up you are inside the game.
You will undergo a series of trials in a game where you can die infinitley. 
Can your body withstand being torn apart over and over? How does your friend fit into all of this? What happens if you do make it out?
Find out yourself by playing through "Crows Are Kind Of Evil" an adventure/game told through the eyes of a new adult missing his friend.
